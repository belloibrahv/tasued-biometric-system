Project Purpose & ScopeDevelopment of a comprehensive biometric identity management system for TASUED integrating facial recognition, fingerprint verification, QR code scanning, and lecture attendance tracking. The system provides multi-factor verification for service access control across campus services (library, hostel, cafeteria, etc.) with real-time monitoring, audit logging, and role-based access for students, lecturers, operators, and administrators. Features include biometric enrollment with liveness detection, QR code generation for lecture sessions, real-time attendance tracking, comprehensive analytics and reporting, secure authentication with JWT tokens, and encrypted storage of biometric data.
